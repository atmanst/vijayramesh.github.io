About Me

I am a Network Engineer with 6+ years of experience at Tata Consultancy Services, working across enterprise network infrastructure, network support, network solution design, and new site infrastructure deployments.

My core strength is network switching and enterprise LAN infrastructure, with practical exposure to VLAN, VTP, STP, HSRP, Layer 2 and Layer 3 technologies, Cisco routers and switches, and network troubleshooting. I work with onsite clients to gather network requirements and collaborate with NOC, SOC, Windows, and cross-functional teams to develop suitable solutions. I am experienced in HLD, LLD, network architecture, network diagrams, project plans, and technical documentation.
